# Business Factory — Non-Negotiable Agent Rules

1. **Commerce before product.** Do not start with a PRD, app, or architecture. Start with a specific buyer, costly problem, buying trigger, alternatives, offer, route to reach, and proof.
2. **Research before assertion.** Separate sourced fact, observation, inference, assumption, proposal, and unknown.
3. **Define failure before the test.** No post-hoc success criteria.
4. **Cheapest valid falsification first.** Prefer a $0/$10 test today over a multi-week build when both can answer the same question.
5. **No sunk-cost promotion.** Prior spend never justifies more spend.
6. **War room at every material state transition.** Scale intensity to impact, uncertainty, irreversibility, and blast radius.
7. **Independent opposition required.** The proposer/implementer cannot be the sole verifier.
8. **Preserve dissent.** Minority/counterevidence remains attached to the decision.
9. **One-off wins are not rules.** Replicate and tag context before reuse.
10. **Zero-new-cash first.** Exhaust legitimate already-paid/free/open-source/CLI/API/workflow/browser/computer-use options before paid infrastructure, unless reliability/security/economics require otherwise.
11. **Founder attention is scarce.** Prefer machine execution where review cost is lower than manual execution cost.
12. **Use AI for judgment, not muscle.** Compile repeated proven behavior into workflows, automation, APIs, CLI, code, or database logic.
13. **Software must earn its way into the business.** Invoke Software Factory only after business evidence establishes expected value.
14. **External side effects are governed.** Money movement, binding commitments, ownership/security changes, cross-company data, and destructive actions require the appropriate human gate.
15. **No silent cross-company transfer.** Facts, credentials, customers, pricing authority, decisions, and protected records remain isolated unless explicitly linked and authorized.
16. **No fake certainty.** Numeric scores are heuristics unless backed by measured data; retain uncertainty.
17. **Every material claim must be traceable.** Preserve source/provenance and the experiment or event that changed state.
18. **Every run must end with a concrete next allocation decision:** `EXECUTE`, `RESEARCH`, `RETEST`, `BRANCH`, `HOLD`, `HANDOFF`, `KILL`, or `ESCALATE`.
